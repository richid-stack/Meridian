# Compliance (needs legal review)

Meridian simulates investing in the prototype. Real community investing requires a licensed partner and legal setup in each market. Nothing in this repo makes the product compliant by itself.

To resolve before any real money moves:
- Which licensed partner and structure (regulatory advice per country).
- KYC / AML provider and retention rules.
- Risk disclosures beside every investment screen (already a design rule).
- Data protection (Ghana and other launch markets; GDPR for EU users).
- Real people and companies only from sourced data, with citations and a corrections process (`Source`, `Correction`).
