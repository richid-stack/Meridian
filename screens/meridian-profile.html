Ama Serwaa Boateng built Kora Freight into West Africa's largest digital freight network, connecting more than 9,000 truck operators with manufacturers across Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire and Nigeria.

She began in logistics at a Tema port operator, then left to fix the problem she saw daily: trucks returning empty. Kora now matches loads in minutes instead of days.
