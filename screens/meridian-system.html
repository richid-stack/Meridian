Meridian

Design system

One language for a product that reads like a newspaper and works like a terminal.

Principles

Editorial surface, terminal coreReading is set in serif on a quiet page. Numbers live in dark panels with mono type.
Rules, not cardsHairlines separate content. A bordered card is reserved for the one action that matters on a screen.
One accentGold marks what is live, linked, or actionable. Nothing else competes with it.
Every fact earns its placeReal content, honest risk, and sources shown next to any AI summary.

Rules

Do

Use sentence case for every label.

Show units and sources beside numbers.

Design loading, empty, and error states.

Keep tap targets at 44px or larger.

Avoid

Gradients, glass, or emoji as icons.

Cards around everything.

All caps labels and arrow suffixes.

A second accent color.
